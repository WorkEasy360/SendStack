A plain-text body. Wrapped in autoescape off because Django autoescapes every
template regardless of extension, which turns the "&" joining the reset link's
two query parameters into an HTML entity. Pasted into a browser, that link
carries a parameter named "amp;token" and the reset fails as "invalid or
expired" -- for a user who did everything right. Nothing here is rendered as
HTML, so escaping buys nothing and costs the link.
